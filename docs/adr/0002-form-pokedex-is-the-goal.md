# The Form Pokédex is the goal, not the app's Pokédex

The app completes its Pokédex species by species, but the players now aim for every form of every species, the Form Pokédex. So Chrono times that goal by default, Boutique and the shop strategy only release a Spare and buy the most selective egg that still holds something missing (down to a plain egg once only common forms are left), and Chance scores new forms, which the app itself never shows. Advice built on the app's species Pokédex would tell a player chasing forms to release Pokémon they still need.
